The product

DigiClips lets journalists and media companies search recorded TV, radio and print coverage by keyword. It's built and maintained by successive university capstone teams.

What I fixed

Searches surviving logout

A user's searches stayed in the browser after logout and showed up for the next person on the same computer. I made logout clear the app's stored search data and saved presets.

Searches returning too few results

When filters removed rows after the database limit was applied, users got fewer results than they asked for. I changed the search API to fetch more rows when filters used up the limit, and added 13 automated tests around it.
